04 / BEFORE YOU BEGIN

GOOD QUESTIONS.
STRAIGHT ANSWERS.

What stats and gear do I need?+

Requirements vary by service. Share your combat stats, available gear and current progress so the exact requirements can be confirmed before a session.

Are the prices shown final?+

No. This is a client design preview, so all prices are placeholders. Final prices and service terms will be confirmed by Ross Services.

How are sessions carried out?+

Services are usually arranged through Parsec. Agree the session details first and review the permissions you grant. Don’t share passwords or recovery codes.

Can I request specific combat achievements?+

Yes—describe the tasks you have in mind and your current progress when discussing a quote. Availability and scope need to be agreed individually.

Can I book through this preview?+

Not yet. You can explore the services and prepare an enquiry, but this preview does not take bookings or payments. The client’s contact destination will be added before launch.
